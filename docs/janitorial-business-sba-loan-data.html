Buying a janitorial company? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 561720 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Commercial cleaning companies are a common first acquisition because they're inexpensive to buy into. Here's what 5,329 SBA 7(a) loans since FY2010, plus 504 and Census data, show about financing one and how those loans turned out.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 68 in FY2010-2019 (median $61,500) and 50 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $154,750). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 5 and 1. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Janitorial loans had a higher loss rate than average: 10.0% charged off by count in FY2010-2019 vs 7.1% for all 7(a) loans, and 4.6% of dollars vs 2.9%. That's the highest of our industry deep dives so far.
  • Loans are small. The FY2010-2019 median 7(a) loan was $75,000 with a median 84-month term; since FY2020 the median is $150,000.
  • Buying an existing company was 11.8% of FY2020+ janitorial 7(a) loans, about the all-industry share (11.0%), with a median acquisition loan of $415,000. Franchise locations got 22% of FY2020+ loans.
  • Payroll per employee was about $30,089 in the U.S. and $30,591 in Utah (2023), the lowest of our deep dives. Census doesn't separate full- and part-time staff, so part-time cleaning shifts likely pull this down (our inference); don't read it as a wage rate.
  • Most establishments are small (77% under 10 employees), but 8,421 U.S. establishments had 20 or more, which is why the average is 16.1 employees. Inference (ours): with low switching costs for customers, contract length and customer concentration deserve extra diligence.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 561720. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
