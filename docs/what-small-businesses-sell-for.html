By sector (where BizBuySell reported it)

  • Service: 992 sales, median price $350,000, median cash flow $157,378.
  • Manufacturing: median price $807,000, average cash-flow multiple 3.02x (highest of the major sectors).
  • Restaurants: 358 sales, median price $235,000, average cash-flow multiple 2.39x.
  • Retail: 417 sales, median price $272,500, median cash flow $119,285.

One broker quoted in the report put it this way: "SDE multiples held steady near 2.0x–3.0x for main street businesses." That's an attributed opinion, not a computed statistic.

How to use this

  • A multiple only means something once you know which earnings figure it's applied to. Confirm how the seller calculated cash flow and add-backs.
  • Smaller deals traded at noticeably lower multiples than larger ones in this quarter.
