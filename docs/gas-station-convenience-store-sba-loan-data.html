Buying a gas station with a convenience store? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 447110 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Gas stations with convenience stores are a common SBA acquisition. Here's what 8,945 SBA 7(a) loans and 1,244 SBA 504 loans since FY2010, plus Census counts, show. SBA files use both the 2017 code (447110) and the 2022 code (457110); we combined them.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 70 in FY2010-2019 (median $584,350) and 33 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $905,200). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 33 and 14. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Losses were below average: 3.3% of FY2010-2019 7(a) loans were charged off by count vs 7.1% for all 7(a) loans, and 1.0% of dollars vs 2.9%. FY2010-2019 504 loans charged off at 2.6% vs 1.5% for all 504 loans.
  • Buyouts are a big share of the business: change-of-ownership loans were 29.7% of FY2020+ 7(a) loans (all industries: 11.0%), with a median acquisition loan of $1,475,000 over a median 300 months.
  • About half of FY2020+ loans (51%, up from 22% in FY2010-2019) carried a franchise code, but the top "franchises" in SBA's FranchiseName field are fuel-supply and branded-reseller agreements (Chevron, Sunoco, ARCO/ampm, Phillips 66), not classic franchises.
  • The median FY2020+ initial rate was 7.25%, and only 7% of loans were fixed-rate.
  • Payroll per employee was about $27,951 in the U.S. and $28,200 in Utah (2023), the second lowest of our deep dives after fitness centers. Utah stations were bigger on average (11.1 employees vs 8.8 nationally) and fewer were under 10 employees (48% vs 68%). Census payroll mixes full- and part-time staff.
  • Inference (ours): the fuel-supply contract's term, pricing and any brand-imaging obligations, plus the environmental condition of the underground tanks, can matter as much as store sales; ask for them early.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 447110. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
