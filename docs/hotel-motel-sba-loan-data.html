Buying a hotel or motel? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 721110 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Hotels and motels are one of the biggest uses of SBA lending, and the loans are large. Here's what 12,709 SBA 7(a) loans and 4,055 SBA 504 loans since FY2010, plus Census counts, show about financing and buying one.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 104 in FY2010-2019 (median $1,352,000) and 40 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $2,251,250). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 67 and 38. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • These are the largest loans in our deep dives: the median FY2020+ 7(a) loan was $2,365,000, and the median change-of-ownership loan was $2,700,000 (all industries: $700,000).
  • Buying an existing property is common: change-of-ownership loans were 33.3% of FY2020+ hotel 7(a) loans vs 11.0% across all industries.
  • Most financed hotels carry a brand: 66% of FY2020+ 7(a) loans had a franchise code, the highest share of our deep dives. The most common brands in SBA's FranchiseName field were Quality Inn, Super 8, Days Inn, Motel 6 and Best Western.
  • Losses: 2.4% of FY2010-2019 7(a) hotel loans were charged off by count (1.1% of dollars) vs 7.1% (2.9%) for all 7(a) loans. FY2010-2019 504 hotel loans charged off at 2.6% vs 1.5% for all 504 loans.
  • Hotels are staffing-heavy: 26.8 employees per establishment in the U.S. and 32.9 in Utah (2023), with payroll per employee of about $39,374 nationally and $31,732 in Utah. Census mixes full- and part-time staff, so that isn't a wage rate.
  • Utah: 636 establishments with employees; 40 7(a) loans since FY2020 (median $2,251,250). Inference (ours): at these loan sizes, a buyer with a budget under about $1 million is mostly looking at small independent or economy-brand motels; check the brand's property-improvement requirements before pricing a deal.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 721110. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
