Buying an auto repair shop? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 811111 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

There are about 84,859 auto repair shops with employees in the U.S. (Census, 2023), most of them small. Here's how SBA-backed loans to general automotive repair shops have been sized and how they turned out.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 146 in FY2010-2019 (median $82,000) and 72 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $250,150). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 52 and 36. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Auto repair loans charged off at 6.4% by count in FY2010-2019, slightly below all 7(a) loans (7.1%), and 2.6% of dollars vs 2.9% overall.
  • Buying a shop is a big part of this industry's SBA lending now: 16.7% of FY2020+ auto repair 7(a) loans were change-of-ownership loans, vs 11.0% across all industries. The median acquisition loan was $611,200.
  • These are small businesses: 90% of U.S. shops with employees had fewer than 10, averaging 4.8 employees. Inference (ours): with that few staff, many shops likely depend heavily on the owner, which matters for a buyer's transition plan.
  • About 8% of FY2020+ auto repair 7(a) loans went to franchise locations, so most financed shops are independents.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 811111. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
