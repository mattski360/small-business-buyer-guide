Buying a roofing contractor? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 238160 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Roofing is a big, fragmented trade: about 25,519 U.S. roofing contractors with employees (Census, 2023), 80% of them with fewer than 10 people. Here's what SBA 7(a) and 504 loan data say about financing and buying one, and how those loans turned out.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 23 in FY2010-2019 (median $75,000) and 28 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $117,000). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 6 and 3. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Roofing had the weakest loan record of our deep dives by dollars: 7.0% of approved FY2010-2019 dollars were charged off vs 2.9% for all 7(a) loans. By count, 10.1% were charged off vs 7.1% overall (about tied with fitness centers for highest).
  • Acquisition loans are large: the median FY2020+ change-of-ownership loan was $955,500, the highest of our deep dives (veterinary clinics are close behind), vs $700,000 across all industries. Buyouts were 9.3% of this industry's FY2020+ 7(a) loans.
  • Older acquisition data is thin: only 28 FY2010-2019 roofing loans carry the "Change of Ownership" label, so don't lean on that column.
  • Franchising is new here: 9.5% of FY2020+ roofing 7(a) loans went to franchise locations, up from 0.3% in FY2010-2019.
  • Utah roofers were a bit bigger on average (9.3 employees vs 8.4 nationally), with lower payroll per employee ($57,224 vs $70,552).
  • Inference (ours): roofing revenue can swing with weather and storm seasons, which may help explain the higher loss rates. Ask a seller for several years of monthly revenue, not just annual totals. The loan data doesn't show this directly.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 238160. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
