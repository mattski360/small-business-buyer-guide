Buying an electrical contractor? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 238210 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Electrical contracting is one of the larger skilled trades, with about 83,342 U.S. contractors with employees (Census, 2023). Here's what SBA 7(a) and 504 loan data say about financing and buying one.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 109 in FY2010-2019 (median $43,000) and 65 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $150,000). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 21 and 16. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • By count, electrical contractor loans charged off at about the all-loan rate: 7.1% in FY2010-2019 vs 7.1%. By dollars the loss rate was higher (4.9% of approved dollars charged off vs 2.9% for all 7(a) loans).
  • Acquisition loans are big. The median FY2020+ change-of-ownership loan was $838,800, above the all-industry median of $700,000 and among the highest of our deep dives (behind roofing and veterinary clinics). Buyouts were 7.8% of this industry's FY2020+ 7(a) loans, below the all-industry share (11.0%).
  • Franchising is rare (4.4% of FY2020+ loans), so nearly every financed contractor is independent.
  • Payroll per employee averaged $76,545 nationally and $66,406 in Utah (2023). Utah contractors were bigger on average (15.6 employees vs 12.2 nationally).
  • Inference (ours): in a licensed trade, the business may depend on the owner's or a key employee's electrical license, so confirm who holds the license and whether that person stays after the sale. The loan data doesn't capture this.
  • SBA 504 loans, which usually finance a building or major equipment, numbered 343 since FY2020 with a median CDC portion of $495,000.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 238210. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
