Buying a child care center? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 624410 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Child care centers are one of the most SBA-financed service businesses. Here's what 10,282 SBA 7(a) loans since FY2010, plus 2,338 504 loans and Census counts, show about financing and buying one.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 71 in FY2010-2019 (median $136,000) and 54 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $473,500). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 30 and 25. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Child care loans held up well: 3.7% charged off by count in FY2010-2019 vs 7.1% for all 7(a) loans, and only 0.8% of dollars vs 2.9%.
  • Buying an existing center is common: 12.6% of FY2020+ child care 7(a) loans were change-of-ownership loans, above the all-industry share (11.0%). The median acquisition loan was $802,000.
  • Startups are a big group too (1,179 FY2020+ loans flagged "Startup"), and franchise locations got 21% of FY2020+ loans.
  • Real estate shows up often (our inference from the loan types): SBA 504 loans, which usually finance a building, numbered 964 since FY2020 with a median CDC portion of $754,000, and the median FY2010-2019 7(a) acquisition loan had a 192-month term.
  • Payroll per employee was about $29,695 in the U.S. and $24,548 in Utah (2023). Census mixes full- and part-time staff, so don't read that as a wage rate. Inference (ours): with staffing ratios set by state licensing rules, labor cost and staff turnover deserve close diligence.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 624410. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
