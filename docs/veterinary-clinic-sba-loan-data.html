Buying a veterinary clinic? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 541940 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Veterinary clinics are bigger than most small businesses (about 14 employees per location on average) and borrow more. Here's what SBA 7(a) and 504 data and Census counts show for veterinary services.

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 36 in FY2010-2019 (median $222,200) and 12 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $1,077,650). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 20 and 6. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Vet loans had the lowest loss record of our three industry deep dives so far: 2.0% charged off by count in FY2010-2019 vs 7.1% for all 7(a) loans.
  • They're large and long. The FY2010-2019 median term was 192 months vs 84 for all 7(a). Inference (ours): terms that long are consistent with real estate being part of many of these loans; the data doesn't say what each loan financed.
  • The median FY2020+ change-of-ownership loan was $953,600, and buyouts were 8.6% of this industry's 7(a) loans.
  • Only 50% of U.S. vet clinics with employees had fewer than 10 people, and 6,799 had 20 or more, so this is a staffing-heavy business compared to auto repair or dental.
  • Utah counts are small (256 establishments; 12 7(a) loans since FY2020). Census flags the Utah employment number as having moderate noise (flag H, 2-5%).

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 541940. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
