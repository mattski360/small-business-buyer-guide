Buying a self-storage facility? What SBA loan data and Census counts show

NAICS 531130 · SBA data as of June 30, 2026 · Census County Business Patterns 2023

Self-storage is a real-estate-heavy business with very few employees. Here's what 1,923 SBA 7(a) loans and 1,017 SBA 504 loans since FY2010, plus Census counts, show about financing and buying a facility (NAICS 531130, lessors of miniwarehouses and self-storage units).

Utah

SBA 7(a) loans with a Utah project location in this industry: 27 in FY2010-2019 (median $975,000) and 23 from FY2020 to June 2026 (median $838,000). SBA 504 loans in Utah: 44 and 29. Small counts, so treat the Utah medians loosely.

What it shows

  • Self-storage loans had the lowest loss record of all our deep dives: 0.6% charged off by count in FY2010-2019 vs 7.1% for all 7(a) loans, and just 0.05% of approved dollars.
  • Many loans fund new construction: 303 of 944 FY2020+ 7(a) loans and 247 of 475 FY2020+ 504 loans were flagged "Startup".
  • Buyouts were 25.5% of FY2020+ 7(a) loans (all industries: 11.0%), with a median acquisition loan of $1,120,000 and a median 300-month term. Inference (ours): a 25-year term is consistent with the loan mostly financing land and buildings; the data doesn't break out what each loan paid for.
  • Franchising is uncommon (8.1% of FY2020+ 7(a) loans), and most of those franchise loans were portable-storage brands (UNITS, MI-BOX, Go Mini's) by SBA's FranchiseName field, not storage-facility brands.
  • Staffing is minimal: 2.6 employees per establishment on average in the U.S. (2.4 in Utah), and 98% had fewer than 10 employees. Census counts only establishments with paid employees, so unstaffed or owner-run facilities aren't in these numbers. Inference (ours): with labor this light, value rests mostly on occupancy, rental rates and the real estate itself.
  • Utah: 205 establishments with employees (2023); 50 7(a) and 73 504 loans since FY2010.

How we calculated this

  • SBA 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan-level files (as of June 30, 2026), filtered to NAICS 531130. Cancelled, undisbursed ("COMMIT") and 504 "NOT FUNDED" loans are excluded.
  • Charge-off rate (count) = loans with status "Charged Off" ÷ loans counted. Charge-off $ rate = gross charge-off amount ÷ gross approved amount.
  • Acquisition loans = loans whose BusinessAge field is "Change of Ownership". Franchise share = loans with any FranchiseCode.
  • Census figures are from the 2023 County Business Patterns national (cbp23us) and state (cbp23st) files, all legal forms of organization.
  • Covers every use of loan funds, not only purchases, unless labeled. Past loan outcomes don't predict any single deal.
